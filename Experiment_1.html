Course Description

Basics of Mechanical & Automation Engg.
The course aims to build interdisciplinary awareness and practical insight into modern engineering applications. It provides all engineering students with a strong foundation in key concepts of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, applicable across domains such as design,manufacturing, thermal, energy systems, automation, and control engineering.
Basics of Electronics Engg.
The objective of this course is to familiarize the prospective engineer with the Fundamentals of analog and digital electronics.
Data Structure
  1. Understanding the basics of computers and programming.
  2. Understanding and application of C++ as a programming language.
  3. Solving basic string and array related problems.
Fundamental of Linear Algebra and Statistics
  1. The objective of a linear algebra course for engineering students is to understand matrix algebra, vector spaces and linear transformations to analyze and solve problems related to linear systems,data analysis, and other engineering applications.
  2. The objective of a statistics course for engineering students is to train them with the statistical tools necessary to analyze data and solving engineering problems.
Soft Skills
The Objective of soft skills is to enhance a person's ability to apply their technical abilities by developing interpersonal and communication skills, which leads to professional and personal success. Courses aim to improve qualities like communication, collaboration, emotional intelligence, adaptability, and problem-solving so individuals can navigate workplace dynamics effectively, build relationships, and achieve career growth.
Design Thinking
The objective of this course is to familiarize students with the design thinking process as a tool for problem-solving. It aims to equip students with design thinking skills and ignite their minds to create innovative ideas, develop solutions for real-time problems
